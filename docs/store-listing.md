# Google Play store listing (draft)

Copy for the Play Console's **Main store listing**, written for the people we expect to play: parents and grandparents (many of them women) who want to do something with the photos and videos
of their family. Plain words, no puzzle jargon, nothing the app does not do. Limits are Google's: title 30 characters, short description 80, full description 4000. Each text is followed by
its length; check it again after editing.

**Say only what is true.** Before pasting, check every line against the app as it will be released (prices, what is free, which puzzles there are). The listing, the in-app text, the
privacy policy and the Data safety form must agree.

## Full description (4000)

```
Turn the photos and videos you love into puzzles.

Pick a picture of the grandchildren, a holiday, a wedding or your garden, and PuzzleVidio cuts it into a puzzle for you. Solve it on your own, or invite your family to solve it with you, wherever they are.

YOUR OWN PICTURES, YOUR OWN PUZZLES
- Make a puzzle from any photo on your phone.
- Make a puzzle from a video, too: the video keeps playing while you solve it.
- A finished puzzle is the picture you love. Save it, look at it, make another.

PLAY TOGETHER
- Start a puzzle and send a link to your family. Everyone works on the same puzzle at the same time.
- A grandmother in one town and a grandchild in another can put the same picture together.
- You see where the others are working and what each person has placed.

SIX KINDS OF PUZZLE
- Jigsaw: classic interlocking pieces, from a quick 50 pieces up to thousands.
- Tile Swap: tap two pieces to trade their places.
- Sliding Tiles: slide the pieces into order.
- Overlap, Strips and Rotascope: fresh twists for when you want something different.

YOU SET THE DIFFICULTY
- Choose a size from small to very large. Start gentle and work up.
- Turn off turning pieces, start with part of the picture already in place, and make pieces snap in as you drag them.
- Stuck? Use a hint to be shown where a piece goes, or a bomb to place a patch of pieces at once.

MADE TO BE RELAXING
- No timer pressure: a clock keeps your best time if you want one, and you can ignore it.
- Gentle sounds and a little confetti when you finish. Sound and animation can be turned off.
- Your puzzles are saved, so you can stop and carry on later.

YOUR PICTURES STAY YOURS
- Puzzles you play on your own never leave your phone.
- A picture is shared only when you start a puzzle with friends, and only with the people you invite.

FREE TO PLAY
- Play on your own for free.
- Watching an optional ad earns extra hints and bombs, or credits for starting puzzles with friends.
- An optional monthly subscription gives you more, and supports the people who make the game.

Questions or ideas? We would love to hear them. Write to us from the link below.
```

## Words to use and words to avoid

- Use: family, together, pictures, photos, videos, relax, grandchildren, memories.
- Avoid: grind, addictive, hardcore, challenge, "casual gamers", and jargon (fast mode, seed, anchor).
- Do not promise what a player may not get: "no ads", "free forever", "works with every video".
